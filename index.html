02 — About

About

Hiroto Ejima is a Japanese composer and audio creator based in the United States.

His work focuses on film music, music direction, spatial audio, and new approaches to sound and music.

Alongside composing, he explores immersive and spatial audio technologies to create new ways of experiencing sound.
